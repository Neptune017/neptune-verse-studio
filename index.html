Peppermint Academy

Demo Available

A cozy western bara visual novel with turn-based RPG combat. Peppermint Academy is a strange school that brings together humans, demons, and angels from many different worlds — colorful and a little chaotic, but the romances are surprisingly personal and grounded.

Behind the art and fantasy setting, this is a game about relationships, emotional baggage, and learning how to connect with people who are not perfect — including yourself. The three main romance interests are Rick, Dimitri, and Jet.

  • Turn-based RPG combat with 8 elements
  • ~30 playable characters to build your party
  • Fully customizable main character
  • Story with multiple endings
  • 12 themed pocket-world missions
  • Nearly 100 collectible custom monsters
  • Crafting, loot, and unlockable abilities
  • Romance routes with personal, grounded stories

The Studio

About Neptune-Verse

I'm Krisztián Csóka from Győrújbarát, Hungary. I've been drawing digitally for about 15 years, and I've held a graphic design qualification since 2017.

My style is shaped by both Japanese anime/manga and American superhero comics — I blend these influences into something uniquely my own.

Recently I've also started working on video game development and 3D modelling, expanding Neptune-Verse into a full creative studio.
