The Studio

About Neptune-Verse

I'm Krisztián Csóka from Győrújbarát, Hungary. I've been drawing digitally for about 15 years, and I've held a graphic design qualification since 2017.

My style is shaped by both Japanese anime/manga and American superhero comics — I blend these influences into something uniquely my own.

Recently I've also started working on video game development and 3D modelling, expanding Neptune-Verse into a full creative studio.
